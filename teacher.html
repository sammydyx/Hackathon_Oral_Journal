How students experience this

  • They receive your question and record a short explanation.
  • They see required ideas they expressed and what to clarify.
  • They submit a second explanation focused on missing ideas.
  • You review class patterns and individual evidence.

Good task design

  • Ask for an explanation, not just an answer.
  • Use 3–5 observable success criteria.
  • List likely misconceptions in plain language.
  • Keep the first response under 90 seconds.
